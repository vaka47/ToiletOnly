# Terms Of Service Draft

Draft only. This is a product draft, not legal advice.

## Eligibility
- Users must be at least `18` years old.
- Users must provide truthful profile information.

## Prohibited Content
- Nudity, exposed genitals, sexual acts, or exploitative content.
- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or stalking.
- Impersonation, fraud, spam, or commercial solicitation without permission.
- Attempts to bypass toilet-only access controls.

## Account and Session Rules
- Access is session-based and may expire after a limited period.
- Matches, likes, and temporary chats may expire by product design.
- Usernames and profile visibility settings may be rate-limited.

## Enforcement
- We may remove content, limit features, suspend accounts, or ban users for violations.
- We may preserve moderation records and related logs to investigate abuse.

## User Responsibility
- Users are responsible for the media and messages they upload.
- Users are responsible for meeting local laws regarding recording and consent.

## Limitation
- The service is provided on an `as is` basis until a final commercial policy is adopted.
