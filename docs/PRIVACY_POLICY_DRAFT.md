# Privacy Policy Draft

Draft only. This is a product draft, not legal advice.

## What We Collect
- Account identifiers used for sign-in.
- Profile data you add: name, age, gender, gender preferences, bio, photos, session videos.
- Device token for push notifications.
- Approximate or precise location when you allow it.
- In-app activity such as likes, matches, messages, reports, and analytics events.

## Why We Use It
- To unlock and operate the social product.
- To show nearby profiles and session videos.
- To deliver matches, messages, and session notifications.
- To moderate harmful or sexual content and handle abuse reports.
- To measure product quality, reliability, and safety.

## Sensitive Content and Safety
- Uploaded media may be processed by automated moderation systems.
- Media can be rejected when it contains nudity, genitals, or unsafe content.
- Reports may be reviewed by authorized moderation staff.

## Retention
- Session videos may disappear from public feed after session expiry but can remain on a user profile until deleted.
- Operational logs and moderation records may be retained longer for fraud, abuse, and legal compliance.

## User Controls
- Edit profile details and delete selected media.
- Block other users.
- Delete profile and request account deletion.
- Control push notifications at the device level.
